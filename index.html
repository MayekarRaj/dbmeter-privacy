Privacy Policy — dBMeter

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

dBMeter ("the app") is a sound level meter application. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Microphone Access

dBMeter requires access to your device's microphone to measure ambient sound levels in decibels (dB). The app processes audio input in real time to calculate decibel readings.

The app does not record, store, transmit, or share any audio data. Only the calculated decibel level (a number) is used — the raw audio signal is never saved or sent anywhere.

Data Stored on Your Device

dBMeter stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Session logs — decibel readings, timestamps, session names, and summary statistics (min/max/average) that you choose to save
  • App settings — your alert threshold and alert on/off preference

This data is stored entirely on your device using local storage and is never transmitted to any server.

Advertising

dBMeter displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data.

Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

dBMeter uses Sentry to collect crash reports and performance diagnostics. If the app crashes, Sentry automatically sends a report containing information about the error, device type, OS version, and app version. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. No personally identifiable information is included in these reports. Please refer to Sentry's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Sharing

dBMeter does not sell, share, or transmit your personal data to any third party, except as described in the Advertising and Crash Reporting sections above.

Children's Privacy

dBMeter does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
